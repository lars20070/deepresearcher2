## Planning

- When in *plan mode*, you are writing the plans as markdown files to `~/.claude/plans/` by default.
- When writing a plan to `~/.claude/plans/` you MUST always copy it to `./.claude/plans/` in the project root. For example, `~/.claude/plans/rosy-dancing-finch.md` should be copied to `./.claude/plans/rosy-dancing-finch.md`.
